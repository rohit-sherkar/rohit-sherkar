About Me

I am a dedicated QA Engineer with over 2 years of experience, specializing in Manual Testing, Accessibility Testing, and Automation Testing using Selenium WebDriver with Java. Currently, I contribute to Meta through Tech Mahindra Limited, focusing on ensuring the highest quality and accessibility standards for their web and mobile applications.

My expertise includes developing and maintaining robust automation frameworks such as TestNG, Page Object Model (POM ), Data Driven Framework, and Hybrid Framework. I am proficient in various testing methodologies, including functional, regression, smoke, and sanity testing, and have a strong background in WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508 compliance testing.

I am passionate about creating inclusive digital experiences and have hands-on experience with screen readers like NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, and TalkBack, as well as accessibility tools such as axe-core, WAVE, and ANDI.
